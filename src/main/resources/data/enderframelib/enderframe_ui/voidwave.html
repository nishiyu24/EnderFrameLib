FAQ

Questions, answered

Do my players need to install anything?

No. Players join with the normal game client and the address you share. Optional cosmetic packs download automatically the first time they connect.

Can I bring my existing world and plugins?

Yes. Upload a world archive and your plugin list and VOIDWAVE validates, converts and boots them in a private staging region before anything goes live.

How does auto-scaling affect my bill?

Plans include a generous baseline. Brief surges are absorbed for free, and sustained growth moves you up one plan, never past the price you agreed to.

What happens if a region goes down?

Players are handed to the nearest healthy region within seconds and their session state follows them, so most never notice a thing.

Can I cancel at any time?

Of course. Export your worlds with one click and your data is deleted from our systems within thirty days.
